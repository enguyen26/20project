Iseltwald Travel Guide

Iseltwald, Bernese Oberland, Switzerland • Updated January 2025

Iseltwald is a tiny village on the southern shore of Lake Brienz that became Instagram-famous after appearing in the Netflix K-drama "Crash Landing on You." Before 2020, it was a quiet lakeside hamlet with a few dozen residents. Now it's overrun with tourists—mostly Korean drama fans—who come for selfies at the iconic pier and then leave. It's beautiful, yes, but it's also a one-hour-max visit unless you're staying overnight.

The reality: Iseltwald has one famous pier, one hotel (the Strand Hotel where the drama was filmed), a handful of houses, and stunning views across the turquoise waters of Lake Brienz to the mountains beyond. That's it. There's no town center, no restaurants worth mentioning, and very little to do beyond taking photos. It's picturesque in the extreme, but the fame has turned it into a photo stop rather than a destination.

What to Know Before You Go

It's very touristy now: The village didn't ask for this fame, and locals are somewhat overwhelmed by the constant stream of visitors. You'll see signs asking tourists to be respectful, keep quiet, and not trespass on private property. The pier itself gets crowded, especially during peak season when tour buses arrive. Early morning or late afternoon offers the best chance for photos without crowds.

There's not much to do: You can walk to the pier, take photos, walk along the lake for 10-15 minutes, and that's about it. The village is tiny—maybe 400 meters from end to end. Unless you're a hardcore K-drama fan making a pilgrimage, one hour is plenty.

Combine it with other stops: Iseltwald works best as a quick detour on your way between Interlaken and Brienz, or as part of a Lake Brienz cruise. Don't make it the centerpiece of your day—you'll be disappointed.

Getting There

Iseltwald is about 15 minutes from Interlaken by car, or 30-40 minutes by bus (route 103 from Interlaken Ost to Iseltwald). The bus runs hourly, and the village has limited parking (which fills up fast in summer). If you're driving, arrive early or late to find parking spots near the lake.

Alternatively, take a boat from Interlaken or Brienz across Lake Brienz—the journey itself is scenic, and you'll arrive directly at the lakefront. Boat schedules are seasonal; check Swiss boat timetables online.

What to Do (It's a Short List)

The Pier: This is the spot. The wooden pier extending into the crystal-clear turquoise water, with mountains in the background. It's as photogenic as the Instagram posts suggest. Expect to wait your turn for photos during busy periods. Be respectful—locals actually live here and are tired of tourists treating their home like a theme park.

Walk along the lake: A short path follows the shoreline with benches overlooking the water. It's peaceful if you can ignore the selfie-taking crowds. The lake color is genuinely stunning—a milky turquoise from glacial silt.

Strand Hotel: The hotel from the drama allows visitors in the lobby and has a lakeside terrace where you can get drinks or a meal (expensive, as you'd expect). If you're a fan, this is your spot. If not, it's just an expensive hotel on a pretty lake.

Harder Kulm viewpoint: Not in Iseltwald itself, but nearby in Interlaken. If you're in the area, the Harder Kulm funicular offers panoramic views over both Lake Brienz and Lake Thun. Worth it if you have extra time.

Where to Eat

Options are extremely limited. The Strand Hotel has a restaurant (expensive, CHF 30-50 for mains), and there's a small snack kiosk near the pier that's open seasonally. Your best bet is to eat in Interlaken or Brienz and treat Iseltwald as a quick photo stop, not a meal destination.

A Few of My Favorite Spots

  • The famous pier on Lake Brienz
  • Lakeside walks
  • Early morning or sunset visits

Practical Information

Timing: Go at sunrise or sunset for the best light and fewer crowds. Midday in summer sees tour buses and large groups—the pier becomes a queue. Weekdays are better than weekends. Avoid July-August if possible, when Korean tour groups arrive in large numbers.

Respect the locals: This is a real village where people live year-round. Don't trespass on private property, keep noise down, and don't block driveways or entrances for photos. The residents didn't choose to become a tourist attraction and are understandably frustrated with the disruption.

Be realistic about expectations: If you've seen photos online, you've basically seen Iseltwald. It's beautiful but small. Don't build your entire Switzerland itinerary around this one spot. It's a nice 30-minute detour, not a destination.

Weather matters: The lake color and mountain views are best on sunny days. Overcast weather turns the water gray and obscures the peaks—the photo magic disappears. Check the forecast before making the trip.

If you're not a K-drama fan: Honestly, skip it unless it's directly on your route. The village is pretty, but Switzerland has hundreds of equally beautiful lakeside spots without the tourist crowds. Brienz, just 15 minutes away, offers similar scenery with better infrastructure and less hype.

The Verdict

Iseltwald is beautiful but overhyped. The K-drama brought it worldwide fame, but that fame has changed the village—it's now a selfie stop rather than a peaceful lakeside retreat. If you're a "Crash Landing on You" fan, it's a pilgrimage worth making. If you're not, spend your time in Brienz, Lauterbrunnen, or one of the region's many other stunning locations that haven't been Instagram-ified into oblivion.

Visit for 30-60 minutes, take your photos, appreciate the scenery, and move on. Don't expect more than that and you won't be disappointed. And please, be respectful to the locals who have had their quiet village turned into a tourist attraction overnight.
